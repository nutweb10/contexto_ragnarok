Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 6, 2026

At RO Monster Hunt (accessible from roguess.online), your privacy is important to us. This document explains the types of information we collect and how we use it to provide a better hunting experience.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number. The information we process includes:

  • Anonymous Game Stats: We record successful hunts, failed hunts, and guess counts to generate global community statistics via Firebase Firestore.
  • Local Preferences: Your language choice (TH/EN) is stored locally on your device via localStorage to maintain consistency.
  • Log Files: Like most websites, we use standard log files provided by our hosting services to monitor traffic and site performance.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads based on your visit to this and other sites on the internet. We use Google AdSense to display advertisements to support the development of this project.

You can choose to opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. [Google Ad Settings]

3. Third-party Services

Our site utilizes professional third-party services to ensure stability and functionality:

  • Firebase (Google Cloud): Used for secure storage of global game intelligence data.
  • GitHub Pages: Our hosting provider for serving site content.

4. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
